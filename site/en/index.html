Your questions.

What is Diqto Voice?

Diqto Voice is a voice translation application designed to let you speak your language and hear the other person in your language during meetings.

Does the other person need to install anything?

No. Diqto Voice runs on your side: the other person stays in their usual meeting application.

Which applications are featured?

Teams, Zoom, Google Meet and Webex, on Windows and Mac. The guides will explain the settings for each platform.

Can I download or buy Diqto Voice here?

Not yet. This website introduces the product; public downloads, sign-ups and purchases are not open. No payment or personal information is requested.

Who is behind Diqto Voice?

DIQTO SAS, a French company based in Marseille. Its legal details are in the legal notice.
